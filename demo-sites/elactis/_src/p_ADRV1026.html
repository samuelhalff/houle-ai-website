ADRV1026: no more hot valves or solenoids

This small, low cost driver is ideal for power reduction and energy saving for all small solenoid valves, relays and electromagnets. A hit and hold circuit decreases the power by a pre-determined amount.

The ADRV1026 will reduce power consumption to 10 % of its original value allowing continous use for over excited coils. Advantages are:

  • No more heat
  • Low cost
  • Small size (10x20mm) with easy integration
  • Special versions available with different parameters and also as a power amplifier
Please, have a look at the datasheet in order to get more technical details about the product.
